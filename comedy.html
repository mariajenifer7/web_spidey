The Wrong Zoom Call

Rahul joined an important online meeting in a hurry. He forgot to turn off his camera and went to the kitchen to make tea. Suddenly, he started dancing while waiting for the water to boil. Unknown to him, the entire board meeting was watching live. The CEO later said: “Best performance we’ve ever seen in a meeting… but please mute next time.”
