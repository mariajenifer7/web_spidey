☎️ The Midnight Call

It was exactly 12:00 AM.

Rain hit the windows hard as Maya sat alone in her apartment. The electricity had gone out an hour ago, leaving only the sound of thunder and the ticking clock in the darkness.

Suddenly...

📞 RING... RING...

Her phone vibrated on the table.

Unknown Number.

Maya hesitated before answering.

"Hello...?"

Silence.

Then a slow whisper came through the speaker.

"Don't look behind you..."

Maya froze.

Her heart pounded loudly in her chest. She laughed nervously, thinking it was a prank call.

But then...

"I'm already inside your house."

The call disconnected.

Maya quickly turned on her phone flashlight and checked every room. Nothing.

She locked the bedroom door and tried calling the police, but there was no signal.

Then she heard it.

TAP... TAP... TAP...

Footsteps outside her bedroom.

Slowly, the doorknob began to turn.

Maya stepped backward, unable to breathe.

Her phone rang again.

Unknown Number.

With shaking hands, she answered.

"I told you not to look behind you..."

Maya slowly turned around.

At first, she saw nothing.

Only darkness.

The faint light from her phone trembled in her shaking hand as she scanned the room. Her closet door was slightly open now. She was sure it had been closed before.

Then—

CREEEEAK...

The closet door slowly opened wider by itself.

Maya stepped back until her legs hit the bed. Her breathing became heavy.

"Who's there?"

No answer.

The room suddenly became freezing cold.

Her phone screen flickered.

Unknown Number Calling...

Again.

Maya stared at the screen in horror before answering.

A raspy voice whispered:

"I can see you."

The bedroom light suddenly flashed on for one second.

And in that single flash—

Maya saw a tall shadow standing inside the closet.

Pale face.

Dark hollow eyes.

A twisted smile.

The light went out again.

Maya screamed and ran toward the bedroom door, but it wouldn't open.

BANG!

Something slammed against the other side.

Her phone slipped from her hand and fell onto the floor.

Through the speaker, the voice started laughing softly.

Then the laughter stopped.

Complete silence filled the room.

Maya slowly bent down to pick up the phone.

On the screen was a photo taken just seconds ago.

A photo of Maya standing in her room.

And behind her...

The smiling figure was getting closer.

Suddenly, icy fingers touched her shoulder.

The phone dropped again.

The last thing Maya heard was the whisper in her ear:

"Now... turn around."

The neighbors reported hearing a loud scream at exactly 12:13 AM.

When the police arrived, the apartment was empty.

No signs of forced entry.

No sign of Maya.

Only her phone remained on the bedroom floor.

Still connected to the call. 📞👁️

👁️ THE END 👁️
