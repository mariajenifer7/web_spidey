The Midnight Knock

At exactly 2:13 AM, someone knocked on Anika’s door. She lived alone on the 12th floor. Nervously, she checked the peephole — nobody was there. Just as she stepped away, another knock came. Louder this time. Her phone buzzed suddenly with a message: “Don’t open the door. I saw someone standing behind you.”

The Smile in the Mirror

Rahul noticed something strange every night. His reflection smiled a second later than he did. At first, he ignored it. But one night, while brushing his teeth, the reflection smiled even though Rahul did not. Frozen in fear, he slowly turned around. Nobody was behind him. Yet in the mirror... the reflection was still smiling.

Room 306

The hotel manager warned Vikram never to enter Room 306. “It’s locked forever,” he said quietly. But curiosity consumed Vikram. At midnight, he stole the key and opened the door. The room looked normal — until he saw dozens of scratches on the walls. Suddenly, the bathroom door creaked open. A pale woman whispered: “You weren’t supposed to come here.”

The Empty Bus

Late at night, Meera boarded the last bus home. She realized she was the only passenger. Every stop passed silently. Then the driver spoke without turning back: “You should not have boarded this bus.” Confused, she looked outside. The streets were unfamiliar. Suddenly, she noticed the passengers reflected in the windows — pale faces staring directly at her.
