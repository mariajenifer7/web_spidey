The Last Message

Aarav waited every night for her message. Even after they broke up, he still checked his phone at midnight. One rainy evening, his screen finally lit up with her name. “I still miss you,” the message read. His heart raced. But before he could reply, another message came: “This number no longer belongs to Priya.” He stared silently at the screen, realizing he had been texting a memory all along.

Love at Platform 7

Every morning, Maya saw the same boy at Platform 7. He always carried a black notebook and smiled whenever their eyes met. Months passed without a single word between them. One day, he finally walked toward her and handed over the notebook. Inside were sketches of her from every morning they had shared the station together. On the last page, he wrote: “I fell in love with the girl who never knew my name.”

Broken Promises

“I’ll come back for you,” Karan whispered before leaving for another city. Nila believed him for three long years. She saved every letter, every photo, every promise. But one evening she saw his wedding picture online. Her world shattered in silence. She closed her laptop, smiled through tears, and finally understood that some people are only meant to teach us how to let go.

The Café Corner

Riya always sat in the same corner of the café every Sunday. A stranger began leaving handwritten notes on her table. Tiny messages like: “Your smile brightens this place.” Weeks later, she finally met him. They laughed, shared coffee, and slowly fell in love. But fate was cruel — he was moving abroad the next day. Sometimes, the shortest love stories leave the deepest scars.
